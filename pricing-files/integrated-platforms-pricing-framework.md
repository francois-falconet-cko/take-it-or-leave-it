## Merchant Breakdown (Standard vs Other MCCs)

### Other MCCs
- Merchants with a chargeback ratio above **1%**
- Or line of business with a CKO risk rating of **Restricted** or **High** as listed in the MCC Risk List

### Standard MCCs
- Line of business with a CKO risk rating of **Medium** or **Low** as listed in the MCC Risk List

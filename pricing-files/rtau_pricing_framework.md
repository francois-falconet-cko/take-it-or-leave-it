### Notes

- Pricing for RTAU is mandatory.
- Free trials are not allowed.

### Approval

- Approval required from the Regional Sales Leader to price below the floor or above the ceiling.

### Standard/Other MCCs

- For more information on the Standard/Other MCCs classification, please refer to Appendix.

### Blended Merchants vs IC++ Merchants

- If a merchant is under IC++ pricing for Card Acquiring (Visa & MC), then RTAU will also be IC++.
- If a merchant is under Blended pricing for Card Acquiring (Visa & MC), then RTAU will also be blended.
- In this case, add the CKO fee from the tables above on top of the Scheme Fees:
  - Visa: $0.12 per update
  - MC: $0.125 per update
